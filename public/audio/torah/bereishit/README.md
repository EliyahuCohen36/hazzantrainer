`1-1.wav` is the master Hazzan recording of Bereishit 1:1. Its word timings and
stressed-syllable timings are in `src/data/sample-curriculum.ts`. If you replace
the recording, update those timings too.

Tips for a good master recording:
- One pasuk per file, starting within ~0.3 s of the first word.
- Quiet room, no music or reverb. Pitch tracking wants a single clean voice.
